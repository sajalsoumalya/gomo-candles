The Story Behind GoMo

Born from a need for everyday calm

Nupur Sinha Jana, working in Kolkata's scented candle space, saw a gap — people knew about mental well-being, but there were few simple, beautiful, affordable ways to actually practise it at home.

She combined two things she believed in deeply: the science of aromatherapy and the Indian cultural tradition of the Evil Eye — that ancient belief in protection from negative energy that every Indian household understands.

Shipping Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Every GoMo order is packed by hand in Kolkata and shipped across India.

Where we deliver

We deliver to most PIN codes in India through our courier partners. If your PIN code can't be served, we'll contact you after you order and cancel it at no cost to you.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on every order. If this changes, the shipping charge will be shown in your cart and at checkout before you place the order.

When your order ships

  • Orders are confirmed and packed within 1–2 working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).
  • Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders we may call or message you to confirm the order before we ship it.

Tracking your order

Once your order ships, you can follow it any time on Track your order using your order number and mobile number. When you're signed in, your orders are also listed under My Account.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available on all orders. Please keep the exact amount ready; our courier partners may not carry change. Refusing a Cash on Delivery order without a valid reason may mean we can't offer Cash on Delivery on future orders.

Damaged or delayed parcels

If the outer box is visibly damaged when it arrives, please take a photo before opening it. If your order hasn't arrived within 10 working days of dispatch, contact us and we'll chase the courier for you. See our Returns & Refunds policy for what happens next.

Questions about delivery? Email support@earthenbeauty.in with your order number.

Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 17 November 2026

We want every GoMo product to bring you a moment of peace. If something isn't right with your order, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If an item arrives broken, leaking, defective, or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we will send a replacement or give you a full refund for that item, at no extra cost.

To help us sort it out quickly, please email support@earthenbeauty.in with:

  • your order number,
  • clear photos of the item and the packaging (an unboxing video helps for breakages), and
  • a short note on what's wrong.

Change of mind

Our candles, sachets and linen mist are fragrance products, so for hygiene and safety reasons we can't accept returns of opened or used items. Unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery; return shipping for change-of-mind returns is paid by the customer.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any time before your order is shipped by emailing us with your order number. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled, but you can follow the return steps above.

Refunds

  • Online payments are refunded to the original payment method.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer to an account you share with us.
  • Refunds are started within 2 working days of us approving the claim (or receiving the returned item). Your bank may take 5–7 working days to show it.

Items we can't refund

  • Opened or used products, unless they arrived damaged or defective.
  • Damage caused after delivery, for example by burning a candle unattended or storing products in heat or direct sunlight (see each product's storage notes).
  • Claims reported more than 48 hours after delivery for damaged or wrong items.

Need help with an order? Email support@earthenbeauty.in. We usually reply within one working day.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information GoMo Candles collects, why, and how you can control it.

GoMo Candles is a brand of Earthen Beauty, Kolkata, India (“GoMo”, “we”, “us”).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number when you create an account or sign in with email or Google.
  • Order details: delivery address, the products you buy, payment method and order history. If you choose to pin your location on the map at checkout, we save that pin to help the courier find you.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • On your device: your cart and sign-in session are stored in your browser so they're there when you come back. We don't use advertising trackers.

We don't collect card or UPI details. If online payment is offered, it is handled entirely by our payment partner (Razorpay).

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including confirming Cash on Delivery orders by phone.
  • To send order updates (confirmation, shipping and delivery) by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers we need to run the shop, and only what they need:

  • courier partners (your name, phone number and delivery address);
  • Google Firebase (sign-in and order database) and Cloudflare (website and server hosting);
  • our payment partner, if you pay online; and
  • government authorities, when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 8 years). Account details are kept until you ask us to delete your account.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can update your name, addresses and contact details yourself under My Account.

Grievance officer

GoMo Candles Grievance Officer · Email: support@earthenbeauty.in. We will acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be shown on the website.

Privacy questions? Email support@earthenbeauty.in.
