# GoMo full review: 26 Nov 2026

Three reviewers read the shop, the admin and the API from a customer's, an owner's and an attacker's point of view, and I screenshotted the live site on a phone. Each finding was traced in the code.

## Already fixed today (live)
- **Account takeover with lookalike emails:** `rahul.k@yahoo.com` and `rahul_k@yahoo.com` shared one database key, so the second could sign in to the first's account or use their staff access. Both now require the exact email. Proven by a test that fails without the fix.
- **Phones:** product-page thumbnails no longer push the page wider than the screen; they scroll sideways.
- **Git:** this folder had been switched to the old `main` branch. Now `main` = the live code, pushed to GitHub.

## Fix next: money, orders, trust (High)

| # | Who it hurts | Problem | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner | When a customer cancels, **you get a "🛎️ New order" alert**, and a late payment on a cancelled order sends the customer "order placed". A packer could pack a cancelled order. | `worker/src/notify.js` admin alert, `orders.js` cancel / restore |
| 2 | Owner | **Cancelling a booked order leaves the Shiprocket pickup live.** The courier comes, and for COD collects cash on a cancelled order. If a cancel happens *during* booking, the order flips back to "packed". | `orders.js` cancel paths, `shiprocket.js` book |
| 3 | Customer | **A failed online payment shows as "Order placed"** in My Account, with no "Pay now" button, then silently cancels 30 min later. | `account.js` orders list |
| 4 | Customer | **Paying in a UPI app on a low-memory phone:** the tab reloads, so the customer returns to a full cart and no confirmation, and may pay again. | `shop.js` (no check of the last order on load) |
| 5 | Customer | **Phone back button leaves checkout** (no step history); tapping outside closes it and restarts at step 1. | `shop.js` modals |
| 6 | Customer | **"Buy now" adds one more unit every tap**, e.g. after closing sign-in. | `shop.js` buy-now |
| 7 | Both | **"Switch to COD" can overwrite a payment that lands at the same moment**, so the customer pays online and then cash. | `orders.js` switchToCod |

## Low
- Cancelling an unpaid online order in the admin messages the customer without asking.
- Dashboard "recent orders" shows "Paid" for orders awaiting a refund.
- Some Shiprocket text on the order page isn't escaped (third-party data).
- Double-click on "Place order" in two tabs at once can still create two orders.
- Coupon use counts can drift (restored order doesn't re-claim; two cancels at once).
- Data export includes staff emails (refund/shipping "by" fields).
- Packers can see revenue on the dashboard.
- Product/offer editor closes without asking if you tap outside with unsaved changes.
- Returns policy says "cancel by email", but there's now a Cancel button.
- Google sign-in doesn't work inside the Instagram/Facebook in-app browser (many Indian buyers arrive that way). Show "Open in Chrome".

## Improvements customers would notice
- Say **when it will arrive** at checkout and on the confirmation ("Ships in 1–2 days, delivered in 3–7").
- Returning customers with a saved address could go straight to Payment.
- "Free delivery above" sits under the COD heading but applies to prepaid too; move it.
- Modals don't trap keyboard focus; the keyboard pops up over checkout on open.
